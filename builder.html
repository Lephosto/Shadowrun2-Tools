With the priority system, Edges and Flaws must add up to zero (SRC p. 22). Edges can’t push a rating past the creation limits, target numbers never drop below 2, and Edges and Flaws with the same effect don’t stack. Buying off a Flaw later costs 10 × its value in Good Karma.
